I’m a multidisciplinary visual creative with a passion for turning ideas into compelling visuals.

My creative journey spans across sketching, photography, videography, video editing, visual storytelling, and AI-powered content creation. Over the past nearly two years of freelance experience, I’ve had the opportunity to work on a variety of creative projects, including commercials, social media content, branded videos, and AI-generated advertisements for clients.

I enjoy working across the entire creative process from developing an idea and creating initial sketches and visual concepts, to capturing footage, designing shots, editing, and bringing the final piece to life. I’m particularly interested in exploring how traditional creative skills and emerging AI technologies can come together to create fresh and engaging visual experiences.

Alongside my freelance work, I’m currently pursuing a B.Sc. in Animation & VFX, where I continue to develop my understanding of animation, visual effects, filmmaking, design, and digital production.

For me, creativity isn't limited to one medium. Whether it's a pencil sketch, a photograph, a camera, an edit, or an AI-generated visual, I’m always looking for new ways to create, experiment, and tell stories through visuals.
